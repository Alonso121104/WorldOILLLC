World OIL Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how World OIL collects, uses, and protects information submitted through our website and mobile oil-change service platform.

1. Information We Collect

World OIL may collect information that customers voluntarily provide when requesting an oil-change service, including name, mobile phone number, service address, vehicle information, appointment information, and other information necessary to provide the requested service.

2. How We Use Information

We use customer information to process and manage service requests, communicate about appointments, connect customers with available technicians, provide service updates, maintain our platform, prevent fraud, and provide customer support.

3. SMS/Text Messaging

If a customer voluntarily opts in to receive text messages from World OIL, we may send SMS messages related to the customer's service request or appointment. These may include request status, technician acceptance, appointment information, completion, and cancellation notifications.

Message frequency varies based on customer activity and service requests. Message and data rates may apply.

Customers may opt out of SMS messages at any time by replying STOP. For help, reply HELP.

4. Mobile Information and SMS Consent

World OIL does not sell or share mobile phone numbers, SMS opt-in information, or SMS consent with third parties or affiliates for their own marketing or promotional purposes.

5. Service Providers

World OIL may use service providers to operate the platform, process payments, provide communications, host data, and support requested services. These providers may process information only as necessary to provide services to World OIL or its customers.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, use, alteration, or disclosure. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

7. Cookies and Website Technologies

Our website and service providers may use cookies or similar technologies to operate the website, maintain functionality, improve performance, and understand how the service is used.

8. Third-Party Services

World OIL may use third-party services such as payment processing, communications, hosting, analytics, or financial-account connectivity. Those services may have their own privacy policies governing information they process.

9. Children's Privacy

World OIL is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Your Choices

Customers may contact World OIL regarding their personal information or SMS preferences. SMS recipients can also reply STOP at any time to stop receiving text messages and HELP to request assistance.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

12. Contact

For privacy or SMS questions, please contact World OIL through the contact information provided on our website.
