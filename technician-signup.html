World OIL Marketplace

World OIL is a marketplace platform that connects customers with independent automotive service providers. World OIL does not perform the automotive repair service.

The provider is responsible for the automotive services they perform and for maintaining any licenses, registrations, permits, insurance, training, and qualifications required for their business and services.

Customer payment for automotive services is handled directly between the customer and the provider under the marketplace model.
