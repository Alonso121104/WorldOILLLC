World OIL Provider Terms

These Provider Terms describe the responsibilities and expectations for technicians participating in the World OIL marketplace.

By registering as a World OIL technician and accepting these terms, the technician acknowledges and agrees to follow the responsibilities described below.

Important: These terms are intended to explain the operating responsibilities of World OIL technicians. They should be reviewed by appropriate legal counsel before being used as a final legal agreement.

1. Communication With Customers

Technicians are responsible for communicating with customers regarding the service they accept.

  • The technician should communicate with the customer before arriving.
  • The technician must obtain and confirm the customer's service address before traveling to the appointment.
  • The technician should confirm the scheduled date and time.
  • The technician should confirm the vehicle information when necessary.
  • The technician should communicate any significant delay or issue to the customer as soon as reasonably possible.
  • The technician should maintain professional communication throughout the service.

2. Service Price

The technician is responsible for communicating the applicable price for the service before beginning work.

  • The technician must clearly communicate the expected service price.
  • The customer should understand what service is being provided.
  • Additional supplies or services should be communicated before they are provided whenever reasonably possible.
  • The technician should not perform unauthorized additional work.
Price transparency: Technicians should make sure the customer understands the applicable charges before work begins.

3. Purchasing Oil and Service Supplies

When a job requires oil, an oil filter, an air filter, or other service supplies, the technician is responsible for obtaining the appropriate items needed to complete the accepted service.

  • The technician may purchase oil, filters, and other required supplies from a retail store, automotive parts store, supplier, or other appropriate retailer.
  • The technician should purchase the required supplies before arriving at the customer's location.
  • The technician initially purchases the required supplies using the technician's own funds.
  • The technician must obtain products that are appropriate for the customer's vehicle and requested service.
  • The technician should keep receipts for purchased supplies.

4. Customer Cancellations and Purchased Supplies

If a customer cancels after the technician has purchased supplies, the technician should retain the purchase receipt.

  • Keep the original receipt whenever possible.
  • Do not throw away receipts for supplies purchased for customer jobs.
  • If supplies can be returned, the technician may follow the retailer's return policy.
  • The technician should keep documentation of the purchase until the cancellation has been resolved.
  • The technician should communicate with World OIL if there is an issue regarding purchased supplies or a customer cancellation.
Technicians should avoid purchasing unnecessary supplies for a cancelled or uncertain appointment whenever reasonably possible.

5. Tools and Equipment

Technicians are responsible for arriving at accepted jobs with the tools and equipment necessary to safely perform the services they accept.

  • The technician must maintain appropriate tools and equipment.
  • The technician should verify that necessary tools are present before leaving for the appointment.
  • The technician should not accept work that the technician is not properly equipped to perform.
  • Damaged or unsafe equipment should not be used.

6. Safety Requirements

Technicians must perform services using appropriate safety practices.

  • Wear appropriate gloves when performing automotive work.
  • Use protective eyewear or safety glasses when appropriate.
  • Use proper precautions around hot engine components and fluids.
  • Use appropriate precautions around moving vehicle components.
  • Keep the work area reasonably safe.
  • Properly secure the vehicle before beginning work.
  • Stop work if an unsafe condition prevents the service from being performed safely.
Safety comes first. A technician should not continue a job when doing so would create an unreasonable safety risk to the technician, customer, vehicle, or other persons.

7. Professional Conduct

World OIL technicians are expected to conduct themselves professionally when interacting with customers.

  • Treat customers respectfully.
  • Communicate professionally.
  • Respect customer property.
  • Maintain appropriate professional behavior.
  • Do not engage in abusive, threatening, or discriminatory behavior.
  • Do not misrepresent qualifications or experience.
  • Only perform services that the technician is qualified and equipped to perform.

8. Completing the Job

Technicians are responsible for properly completing the services they accept.

  • Perform the agreed service using appropriate procedures.
  • Verify that the work has been completed properly.
  • Clean the immediate work area after completing the service.
  • Properly handle used oil and other automotive waste.
  • Communicate completion of the service to the customer.
  • Provide applicable information regarding the service or supplies when appropriate.

9. Charges for Supplies and Work

After the job has been completed, the applicable charges for approved oil, supplies, and technician work should be communicated to and charged to the customer according to the applicable World OIL service arrangement.

Technicians should maintain accurate records and receipts for supplies purchased for customer services.

  • Do not misrepresent supply costs.
  • Keep receipts for purchased supplies.
  • Clearly communicate applicable charges.
  • Do not add unauthorized charges.

10. Technician Service Address

World OIL may require a technician to provide a service address when creating or maintaining a technician account.

The service address may be used for purposes such as determining the technician's service area and matching the technician with customer jobs.

The technician should keep the service address accurate and updated when necessary.

A technician's service address is intended for operational purposes and should not be publicly displayed unless otherwise authorized.

11. Responsibility for Damage

Technicians are expected to use reasonable care when working on customer vehicles and property.

The technician may be responsible for damage caused by negligence, improper procedures, failure to follow applicable safety requirements, or failure to use appropriate tools or equipment.

  • Use appropriate procedures.
  • Use appropriate tools.
  • Follow vehicle specifications and applicable service requirements.
  • Take reasonable care to protect the customer's vehicle and property.

12. No Guarantee of Jobs or Earnings

World OIL does not guarantee that a technician will receive a specific number of customer appointments, jobs, customers, or earnings.

The availability of customer work may vary based on customer demand, location, technician availability, service requirements, and other factors.

13. Technician Responsibilities

By participating as a World OIL technician, the technician acknowledges responsibility for:

  • Communicating with customers.
  • Obtaining and confirming customer service addresses.
  • Communicating applicable service prices.
  • Obtaining required oil and supplies.
  • Initially paying for required supplies with the technician's own funds.
  • Keeping receipts for purchased supplies.
  • Arriving with the required tools and equipment.
  • Following appropriate safety practices.
  • Using gloves and protective eyewear when appropriate.
  • Performing accepted services professionally.
  • Properly completing the accepted work.
  • Communicating applicable charges to the customer.
  • Taking reasonable care to prevent damage to customer vehicles and property.

14. Agreement

By checking the acceptance box during technician registration, the technician acknowledges that the technician has read these Provider Terms and agrees to follow the responsibilities described in them.

The technician understands that failure to follow applicable World OIL requirements may affect the technician's ability to participate in the marketplace.

Legal review recommended: These terms should be reviewed and finalized by a qualified attorney before being used as the company's final legally binding provider agreement, particularly with respect to California law, technician classification, customer payments, refunds, liability, insurance, consumer protection, and marketplace requirements.
